I’m Wren M. Burton (she/her), a PhD student in Rehabilitation Sciences.

I’ve worked clinically with older adults in pain management, where I continue to see gaps in care. My research asks how considering frailty alongside chronic pain could help shape more thoughtful care pathways, including the role of nonpharmacologic interventions.
